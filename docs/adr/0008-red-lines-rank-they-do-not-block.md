# 8. Red lines rank, they do not block

A red line — a condition the reader has declared unacceptable in advance —
promotes matching clauses to the top of the ranking and marks them as hitting
something the reader named. It does not block, veto, or produce a walk-away
recommendation.

## Why

Red lines were conceived for documents you can negotiate. Against an adhesion
contract (ADR 0002) a red line has nothing to push against, so its only honest job
is to change what the reader sees first. Turning it into "this document breaks two
of your red lines, do not sign" would reintroduce the verdict rejected in ADR 0007
by another route.

## Consequences

- Red lines change presentation, not detection. The same clauses are found either
  way, so a reader who sets none loses no coverage.
- This is the only per-reader variation in the output.
