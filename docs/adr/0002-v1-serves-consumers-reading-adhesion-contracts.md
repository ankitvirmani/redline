# 2. v1 serves consumers reading adhesion contracts before signing

Redline v1 is for an individual reading a take-it-or-leave-it document — a terms
of service, subscription, gym membership, or offer letter — at the moment before
they accept it. Freelancers, small business owners, startup founders, and tenants
are deliberately not served in v1.

## Considered options

The research surfaced four viable segments. Small business owners had the
strongest evidence of willingness to pay ($2K–$13.3K/yr legal budgets; LegalShield
business tiers at $59.95–$169.95/mo), and freelancers were the only segment where
every proposed feature worked at once. We chose consumers on adhesion contracts
because that is where the harm evidence is strongest by a wide margin: arbitration
and class-action waivers, auto-renewal, fee escalators, and non-competes are the
only clause types in the research carrying federal-regulator measurement (CFPB,
FTC), and all four appear in documents nobody negotiates.

## Consequences

- **Counter-offer drafting is cut from v1.** You cannot counter-offer a gym
  contract. Removed from the `CLAUDE.md` capability list on the owner's
  instruction, 2026-08-29.
- **Willingness to pay is the weakest part of this choice.** No source found a
  consumer paying to have a terms of service explained, and ToS;DR does a free
  version of the same job for platform terms.
- **"Red lines" weaken too.** A declared red line is actionable when you can
  negotiate; against an adhesion contract it can only advise walking away.
