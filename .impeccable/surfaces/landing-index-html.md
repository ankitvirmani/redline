Scope: the Redline landing page, the root route. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: a reader about to accept an adhesion contract they cannot negotiate,
minutes from committing, not a lawyer, with no counsel. `PRODUCT.md` owns the
rest.

Job on this surface: understand in seconds that Redline shows the sentence behind
every risk it names, and reach the paste box.

Action: one. Paste a document.

Proof: the mechanism itself, demonstrated on a document. Every claim traces to the
landing-page claims list in `PRODUCT.md`. No accuracy figure, no testimonial, no
customer, no price, no verdict, no enforceability claim, no scanned-document
support.

Constraints: WCAG 2.2 AA. Severity never carried by color alone. The glossary in
`CONTEXT.md` governs every word: reader, flag, source sentence, red line,
severity, consequence. All copy runs through the humanizer skill before commit.
No dependency added without the owner's approval.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A contract is small, plain and businesslike; the warnings that land on it
are enormous. This page owns the power relationship between the two, and the
modules that carry a warning appear twice, once as a bar in the margin and once as
the same bar under the exact sentence it came from, so the citation is a visible
physical fact rather than a promise. It refuses the category hero: no
soft-shadowed screenshot floating on a gradient, no trust-navy, no feature-card
row, no metric strip, because Redline has no metrics and borrowing the shape of
proof is the thing this product exists not to do.

OWN-WORLD: Studio Dumbar's modular identity. Cool grey rail against a true white
reading field, ink black type, and four flat unmixed inks that carry no severity
meaning of their own: cyan #00B7FF, magenta #FF2DA1, yellow #FFD400, green
#32D74B. Oversized hard-edged bars snap to a module; small rotated fragments of
the same inks scatter and lock. One grotesque, Archivo, set small and workmanlike
for prose and enormous for display. Black on every ink, white only on ink black.
No gradient, no tint, no glass, no shadow as decoration. Recognizable with every
word removed by the bar module alone.

STORY: The reader sees a contract with three bars struck across it, reads one
consequence in plain language, follows the bracket to the sentence that produced
it, and understands that the claim and its source are the same object. Then they
learn what Redline refuses to do, which is the part a chatbot cannot match. Then
they paste their own document.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A grey rail on the left, roughly a fifth of the width, carrying
the black square mark, the product name, one sentence, the cyan primary action
with its arrow, and beneath a hairline the seven clause types it checks. The rest
is white and holds the demonstration: an example subscription agreement set at
reading size in black, with three oversized bars landing across it in magenta,
yellow and cyan, each carrying a severity word in caps, its consequence in one
line, and a short code ruled beneath. A black keyline drops from each bar and
brackets the one sentence it came from, and that sentence carries the same ink as
a hard bar beneath it. The primary action sits in the rail, at the top, above the
fold on every width.

FORM: Modular Bars, from the Studio Dumbar identity system. A dealt challenger,
chosen by the owner over the assigned direction and over my own pick; candidate
position not applicable, it came from the catalog rather than my grounded list.
Seed key 184bd34d.
