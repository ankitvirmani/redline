# Brief: ticket 14, the landing page

This is the one surface whose job is persuasion rather than analysis, which makes it
the easiest place in the product to breach the rule the rest of it is built on. Read
the ticket's own warning about that before you write a sentence.

Read, in this order:

1. `.scratch/redline-v1/AGENT-BRIEF.md` in full.
2. `.scratch/redline-v1/issues/14-landing-page.md`. Your ticket. It is longer than
   the others and every paragraph is a constraint.
3. `PRODUCT.md` in full. The "Landing-page claims list" under "Evidence on Hand" is
   the authority for every sentence on the page, and the "Absences future work must
   not fabricate" list immediately above it is what you may not fill in.
4. `PRD.md` section 4, the paragraph "Landing-page claims are grounded".
5. `.scratch/redline-v1/spec.md`, the paragraphs beginning "The landing page touches
   no seam", "Landing-page claims are grounded the same way a flag is", "The claims
   list is the authority, not taste", and "The landing page owns the root route".
6. `docs/adr/0005-jurisdiction-neutral.md` and
   `docs/adr/0007-what-a-flag-shows.md`.
7. `CONTEXT.md` in full. Every word on this page is governed by it, avoid-lists
   included. Reader, not user.
8. `DESIGN.md` from `# Design System: Redline` onward, in full. This page is where
   the display register, the whole-field colour sections, the scattered fragments, the

9. `.impeccable/surfaces/landing-index-html.md`, the surface brief the shipped page
   was built from.

## What already exists

The shipped landing page, ported to `app/landing/page.tsx` with `app/landing.css` and
`components/LandingRuntime.tsx`. It is a faithful port of `landing/index.html`, which
`DESIGN.md` is recorded from. It was written and humanized before it was committed and
it already obeys the claims list.

**Start from it. Do not rewrite it.** Your job is not a new landing page. It is three
changes to an existing one, plus an audit.

## The three changes

**1. Move it to the root, and move the paste box to its own route.** The page owns
`/` and the paste box, currently at `app/page.tsx`, moves to its own route. Pick the
route name and say why; `/analyse` is the obvious candidate and matches the product's
own vocabulary. Every link, every redirect and the skip link's target follow. The old
`/landing` path should not linger as a duplicate: decide whether it redirects or goes
away, and say which.

The paste box must still be reachable in one step from the landing page. One step means
one click or one keypress from the page's primary action, with no interstitial.

**2. Replace the fold's demonstration with real rendered output.** The criterion is
"Product imagery shows real rendered output from ticket 08, never a mockup."

The shipped page's fold shows a document with three source sentences and three flag
bars. That content was written by hand before any analysis existed. Now the pipeline
works, so run `tests/fixtures/adhesion-contract.txt` through it and use what actually
comes back: the real flags, their real severity bands, their real confidences, their
real consequences, and their real source sentences quoted verbatim from the fixture.

Use `npm run smoke`, which ticket 10 wrote to print exactly this. If the real model is
unavailable to you, use the deterministic path through the stub, which is built from
the fixture sidecar and is therefore real output of the real render path over a real
document, and say in your report which one you used. Either way the sentences shown
must be verbatim substrings of `tests/fixtures/adhesion-contract.txt`, and you should
write a test that asserts it, because that is this page's version of the rule the whole
product rests on.

Keep the "Example document" truth-guard chip. It is honest and it is load-bearing: the
document shown is a fixture, not a real reader's contract, and the page says so.

Do not change the fold's layout, its register, its two-column stage, its bar overhang
or its runtime keyline to accommodate different content. Fit the content to the design.
If a real consequence is too long for the bar, that is a real constraint the product
has and the honest fix is in the content, not in loosening the design.

**3. Audit every sentence against the claims list.** Go through the page line by line.
For each sentence, name the entry in `PRODUCT.md`'s landing-page claims list it traces
to. Produce that mapping as a table in your report, every sentence accounted for.

A sentence that traces to no entry needs a decision. The owner is absent, so the
decision is yours and the ticket tells you which way to lean: the claims list is the
authority, not taste. Cut it or bring it inside an entry, and record what you did and
why.

While you are in there, check the page against the "Never, until something changes"
list and the absences list:

- No accuracy, recall, precision, success rate or benchmark figure. Anywhere. Not
  hedged, not qualified, not "early results suggest".
- No testimonial, customer name, reader count, review or case study. None exist.
- No comparative quality claim against any product, named or unnamed. "Better than a
  chatbot" is the same untested capability claim as a number, without the number. The
  chatbot comparison is permitted at mechanism level only: what each tool structurally
  can and cannot do.
- No price, plan or availability claim.
- No legal advice, no verdict, no enforceability claim.
- Nothing about how readers arrive, what they searched for, or what they were doing
  beforehand. `PRODUCT.md` records that nothing is known about this and that the gap
  stays recorded rather than filled in.

The page must state that Redline does not give legal advice and does not account for
where the reader lives, and it must never tell a reader whether to sign, in words or by
implication. Check the implications as hard as the words: a page that says "know what
you are agreeing to before you accept" is fine, and one that says "do not sign until
you have read this" is a verdict.

## Tests, `tests/landing.test.ts`

- Every source sentence rendered on the page appears verbatim in
  `tests/fixtures/adhesion-contract.txt`. This is the page's version of ADR 0001 and it
  is the most valuable test in this ticket.
- No accuracy figure appears in the committed copy. Write a check you can defend: a
  pattern over percentages, ratios and words like recall, precision, accuracy and
  benchmark, applied to the page's text. Say in your report what it catches and what it
  would miss.
- The page renders with no model call, no call to the analysis path, and no auth: fail
  if `fetch` is called, and assert nothing on the page imports a seam or a Supabase
  client.
- The landing page is served at the root and the paste box at its own route. Assert on
  the routes that exist, not on a link's text.
- The legal-advice statement and the jurisdiction-neutrality statement are present.
- The paste box is reachable from the page: the primary action's target is the paste
  box's route.

## Design

The page is already designed and `DESIGN.md` is recorded from it, so the discipline
here is restraint. Do not restyle, do not modernise, do not add a section, do not add a
feature-card row, a metric strip or a product screenshot with a soft shadow.
`DESIGN.md` names those three specifically and says the fold's refusal of them is
load-bearing, because Redline has no metrics and will not borrow the shape of proof.

Two things `DESIGN.md` records as known limits in the shipped artifact, which you may
fix if your changes touch those areas and should otherwise leave alone:

- The anatomy list and the question box read closer to documentation than to the world
  the fold establishes. Recorded as a defect the build carries. A new surface should
  follow the fold's register, not those two sections.
- The overprint bar appears on only two headings, which is an inconsistency rather
  than a rule that the device is rare. Applying it to more headings is consistent with
  the system.

WCAG 2.2 AA, which the ticket lists as a criterion and the shipped page was built for.
Verify rather than assume: check contrast on every ink pairing you touch, check the
focus order through the page including the skip link, check that the runtime keyline
and the scattered fragments are `aria-hidden`, check the page at 400px wide, and check
it with JavaScript off, since `DESIGN.md` says the default state is already visible and
nothing waits on script. Report what you checked and how.

## Copy

All copy runs through the `humanizer` skill before it is committed. That is a standing
rule in `CLAUDE.md` and this ticket restates it as a criterion.

Run the skill over every sentence you write or change, not the whole page, because the
existing copy already went through it and rewriting humanized copy tends to make it
worse. Where you cut a sentence for tracing to no claim, you do not need the skill; where
you write a replacement, you do.

`CONTEXT.md` governs every word: reader, document, flag, source sentence, severity,
confidence, completeness, clean document, consequence. Its avoid-lists are binding, so
no user, no file, no issue, no finding, no citation, no excerpt.

Say in your report that you ran the skill and on which strings.

## Criteria, restated

- [ ] A reader who has never used Redline can tell what it does from the first viewport.
- [ ] The page states what makes Redline different from pasting a document into a chatbot, at mechanism level only, with no claim of being more accurate, more reliable, or better.
- [ ] The paste box is reachable in one step from the page.
- [ ] Every sentence on the page traces to an entry in the `PRODUCT.md` landing-page claims list.
- [ ] Every external claim carries a citation that resolves to a real source stating that fact.
- [ ] No accuracy figure, success rate, or performance benchmark appears anywhere in the copy.
- [ ] No testimonial, customer name, user count, review, or case study appears, because none exist.
- [ ] Product imagery shows real rendered output from ticket 08, never a mockup.
- [ ] The landing page is served at the root route and the paste box has moved to its own.
- [ ] The page states that Redline does not give legal advice and does not account for where the reader lives.
- [ ] The page never tells a reader whether to sign, in words or by implication.
- [ ] The page renders with no model call, no call to the analysis path, and no auth requirement.
- [ ] Copy has been run through the humanizer skill before commit.
- [ ] Meets WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Out of bounds

No new marketing section. No signup, no waitlist, no email capture, no pricing, no
analytics, no tracking script, no third-party embed. No model call from this page,
ever. No change to a seam. Do not start an Impeccable direction round; it opens a
browser and waits for a person, and no person is here.

Do not touch `landing/` (it stays as the design source of record), `tests/fixtures/`,
`docs/`, `PRD.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, or anything under
`.scratch/`. Do not commit.
