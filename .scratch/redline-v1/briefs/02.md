## What already exists

Ticket 01 shipped: a Next.js app at the repository root, a paste box at `app/page.tsx`
that renders pasted text back losslessly with a character count, Vitest wired behind
`npm test`, and `src/domain/text.ts`. Read those three files before you change them.

## What to build

Pasted text stops going straight to the screen and passes through the extraction
seam instead. The seam is the first of the four in the spec: input in, and out come
the extracted text, the kind of source it came from, and a completeness assessment.
The reader sees the completeness reading on every analysis.

Put it at `src/extraction/`. Its public surface is one function and a set of types.
Nothing downstream should need to know whether the input was pasted or parsed from a
PDF beyond the `sourceKind` field, because ticket 03 adds PDFs behind this same seam.

## How to do it

**Types, in `src/extraction/types.ts`.** An extraction result carries the extracted
text, the source kind, and the completeness assessment. Ticket 03 will add a typed
rejection alongside the success case, so make the return type a discriminated union
from the start rather than something ticket 03 has to break. Model the rejection
case now with a machine-readable reason code, even though pasted text has no
rejection path yet, and leave the reason codes ticket 03 needs out of it; do not
invent PDF behaviour.

**Losslessness is the load-bearing property.** Extracted text is character-identical
to the input. No trimming, not even leading or trailing. No whitespace collapsing.
No smart-quote, ligature or dash normalisation. No line-ending rewriting. No Unicode
normalisation of any form: do not call `normalize()`. Everything downstream compares
model-returned spans against this text, and any normalisation breaks every citation
silently, which is the failure this seam exists to prevent.

**Completeness, in `src/extraction/completeness.ts`.** Assessed from structural
signals only, per the spec:

- the text ends mid-sentence
- there is no closing or signature block
- the length is implausible

Type-relative plausibility is explicitly deferred; it needs a document type that
only analysis determines, and these signals already catch the case that matters,
which is a reader pasting part of a page.

Decide what the assessment looks like to a reader and to code, and record your
reasoning in your report. It needs to be a real reading rather than a boolean, because
the reader has to be able to tell a thorough result from one run on a fragment, and
it needs to be deterministic and testable. Whatever shape you choose must carry the
signals that fired, not only a verdict, because a reader who is told the analysis may
have received part of the document deserves to know what made Redline think so.

Do not make this a model call. This seam has none.

**The reader sees it on every analysis.** Not only when it is low. A reading that
appears only on bad documents teaches the reader nothing on good ones and looks like
an error when it does appear. It informs and it gates nothing: a low reading
suppresses nothing, hides nothing, and blocks no submission. That behaviour is
recorded under ADR 0006 as a known gap. Implement the recorded behaviour; do not
resolve the question.

**Tests, `tests/extraction.test.ts`.** No model call, no network.

- Character-level fidelity: the extracted text equals the input exactly, over
  `tests/fixtures/adhesion-contract.txt` read from disk and over a hand-built string
  carrying curly quotes, a ligature glyph, a non-breaking space, a tab, double
  spaces, trailing whitespace and an astral character. Assert exact equality.
- Fidelity of the pathological inputs: a string that is only whitespace, a string
  with no trailing newline, a string with a leading newline, the empty string.
  Whatever the seam does with the empty string, it must not be to silently return
  success with empty text; decide and justify.
- Truncation: the adhesion contract truncated at 40% of its characters produces a
  visibly different completeness reading from the same document intact. "Visibly
  different" means a reader would see a different reading on screen, so assert on
  the field the screen renders, not on an internal score that happens to differ.
- Each structural signal fires on an input built to trigger it and stays quiet on
  one built not to. The signature block signal is worth care: the adhesion contract
  ends with a real closing and signature block, and the clean document's ending
  differs, so use both.
- A low completeness reading blocks nothing. Assert that the seam returns text and a
  usable result for a badly truncated document rather than a rejection.

**The screen.** Show the reading where the reader will see it on every analysis,
in the Operate register from the app-shell brief. Follow `DESIGN.md`: zero radius,
the tab-bar motif where a line needs attaching to something, the label register for
the reading's key, a 1px grey hairline to separate rather than a card. Severity and
completeness are different signals and must not look like each other; completeness is
not a flag and gets no flag ink. Do not use colour alone to carry it, and do not use
green to mean good: `DESIGN.md` records that this system never uses green that way.
Motion 150 to 250ms, state only.

**Copy.** The reading's label and its explanation are reader-facing copy and run
through the `humanizer` skill before you finish. `CONTEXT.md` says completeness, not
coverage, quality or parse rate. Say what the reading means in the reader's terms:
how much of the document Redline believes it received. Do not apologise, do not
alarm, and do not imply the reader did something wrong. Say in your report that you
ran the skill.

## Criteria, restated

- [ ] Extraction returns extracted text, source kind, and a completeness assessment.
- [ ] Extracted text is character-identical to the input, with no normalisation of whitespace, quotes, ligatures or line breaks.
- [ ] A document truncated at 40% produces a visibly different completeness reading from the same document intact.
- [ ] The completeness reading is shown to the reader on every analysis.
- [ ] A low completeness reading blocks and hides nothing.
- [ ] Fidelity and truncation detection are covered by tests that make no model call.

## Out of bounds

No PDF parsing and no `pdfjs-dist` import: that is ticket 03, and it is the one that
settles how a PDF reaches this seam. No analysis, no flags, no model call, no
ranking, no auth, no storage. Do not touch `app/landing/page.tsx`, `landing/`,
`tests/fixtures/` (read them, do not edit them), `docs/`, `PRD.md`, `PRODUCT.md`,
`DESIGN.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, or anything under `.scratch/`. Do not commit.
