## What to build

A document where no flag met the bar reads as clean, together with the list of clause
types that were checked.

The determination is made in the ranking seam, `src/ranking/`, which ticket 06 built
with room for it. It is model-free and tested there.

## The four things that matter

**1. Never a blank result.** An empty flag set yields a clean-document
determination, not an empty list rendered with nothing in it and not a silent
success. A reader who sees an empty screen cannot tell a clean document from a failed
parse, and that confusion is the specific failure this ticket exists to prevent.

**2. The checked-list is what makes "clean" mean anything.** The clean result names
every clause type that was checked, all seven, by name. Not a count. Not "we checked
for the usual clauses". The named list is the evidence that work happened.

Where the list comes from matters: it must be the clause types the analysis actually
checked, carried through from analysis, rather than a constant the render path prints.
If those two ever diverge, the constant would lie. Wire it from the seam.

**3. Nothing is invented to look useful.** A tool that manufactures a low-severity
finding on a benign document has failed even though nothing it said was false. The
clean document fixture is the test: it must produce zero flags, at every band, not
just zero at the top band. If the stubbed path produces a flag on it, that is a
defect to report rather than a threshold to tune.

**4. A low completeness reading appears beside a clean result and does not suppress
it.** This is the recorded behaviour under ADR 0006 and it is a known gap, not an
oversight. Implement the recorded behaviour. Do not resolve the question. Do not add a
condition that hides the clean verdict when completeness is low, and do not soften the
clean verdict's wording based on completeness, which would be the same suppression by
another route. The two readings sit side by side and the reader combines them.

Say in your report that you implemented the recorded behaviour and that you did not
resolve the open question, so the owner does not have to go looking.

## Tests, `tests/clean-document.test.ts`

Pure-function tests in the ranking seam. No model, no network, no database.

- An empty flag set yields a clean-document determination carrying the full
  checked-list. Assert on the named types, not on a count.
- A flag set with one moderate flag does not yield a clean determination. The bar is
  "no flag met it", so one flag anywhere means not clean.
- The checked-list that comes out equals the checked-list that went in. Pass a short
  list and assert you get that list back, so a hardcoded seven-entry constant fails
  this test.
- A clean determination with a low completeness reading is still a clean
  determination, and the completeness reading is still present on the result. This is
  the ADR 0006 behaviour asserted directly.
- End to end against the clean fixture through the stub: zero flags at every band,
  and a clean determination with the checked-list. Assert zero at every band rather
  than zero at the top, because the ticket's own wording is about no invented finding
  at all.
- A clean determination is distinguishable in the returned data from a refused or
  failed analysis. These are three different outcomes and a consumer must be able to
  tell them apart without string matching on copy.

## The screen

A designed state, per the app-shell brief. Three outcomes have to be visibly
different from each other: clean, refused, and failed. Clean is not an absence.

`DESIGN.md` constrains this more than it might seem. **Do not use green to mean
good.** The file records explicitly that Check Green is a fourth identity slot and
that the build never uses it to mean safe or pass. Do not use a tick, a checkmark
glyph, or any icon font; icons in this system are inline SVG with stroke-width 2 to
2.2 and square caps. No badge, no card, no reassuring colour field.

What the system does give you: the full-bleed field, the 2px ink keyline, the
tab-bar motif for a line that attaches to something, the label register for the
checked-list's keys, and the 1px grey hairline for separating rows. The checked-list
is a real list of seven named things and the honest way to render it is as one, in
the register `DESIGN.md` gives rail list items, each with the small ink marker that is
the bar motif at its smallest.

The completeness reading sits beside the clean verdict, in the shape ticket 02 built.
Do not restyle ticket 02's component; reuse it.

WCAG 2.2 AA. The clean verdict is conveyed in text, not by colour or an icon, and a
screen reader reaching this region learns both that nothing met the bar and what was
checked.
